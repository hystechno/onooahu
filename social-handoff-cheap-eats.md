## Instagram Caption — "Your Oahu Guide"

🌺 POV: You just ate the best meal of your trip for under $15 👇

Oahu's best food was never behind a resort gate — it's at the counters, windows, and bakery cases locals have lined up at for decades. Here are our 10 favorite cheap eats on the island (every plate under $15!):

1️⃣ 🍳 Rainbow Drive-In, Kapahulu — the plate lunch blueprint since 1961. Get the mix plate or loco moco, extra gravy.
2️⃣ 🍜 Marukame Udon, Waikiki — fresh noodles made in front of you, bowls from ~$7. The best $10 in Waikiki.
3️⃣ 🥩 Helena's Hawaiian Food, Kalihi — a James Beard winner where dinner costs less than a movie ticket. Pipikaula short ribs!
4️⃣ 🐟 Alicia's Market, Kalihi — poke like a local. Spicy ahi + crispy pork belly plate, takeout only.
5️⃣ 🌶️ Zippy's, island-wide — the 24-hour safety net. Chili rice, oxtail soup, and a coco puff to finish.
6️⃣ 🥟 Mei Sum Dim Sum, Chinatown — har gow, siu mai & baked char siu bao, all day, all a few dollars a basket.
7️⃣ 🍩 Leonard's Bakery, Kapahulu — hot malasadas for about $2 each. The best dollar-to-delight ratio on Oahu.
8️⃣ 🌯 Kono's Northshore, Haleiwa — the breakfast bomber stuffed with 12-hour kalua pig. Post-surf fuel.
9️⃣ 🥧 Ted's Bakery, Sunset Beach — chocolate haupia cream pie for breakfast. No regrets.
🔟 🍧 Matsumoto Shave Ice, Haleiwa — the $5 icon since 1951. Add ice cream + a snow cap, trust us.

💡 Pro tip: bring cash, go early, and split the big plates — portions are huge.
