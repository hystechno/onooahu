## Instagram Caption — "Your Oahu Guide"

🌺 WHAT TO EAT ON OAHU — the 10 iconic dishes that define the island, and exactly where to find each one 👇

A century of Hawaiian, Japanese, Portuguese & Filipino cooking collided here and made foods you can't get anywhere else. The checklist:

1️⃣ 🍩 Malasadas — Leonard's Bakery, Kapahulu. Hot, sugar-dusted, ~$2. Get the li hing.
2️⃣ 🦐 Garlic Shrimp — Giovanni's Shrimp Truck, Haleiwa. The original. Messy, buttery, mandatory.
3️⃣ 🥧 Hula Pie — Duke's Waikiki. Mac nut ice cream mountain on the sand. Built to share (in theory).
4️⃣ 🥩 Pipikaula Short Ribs — Helena's Hawaiian Food, Kalihi. James Beard-recognized since 1946. Tue-Fri only!
5️⃣ 🍫 Coco Puffs — Liliha Bakery. The 24-hour obsession. Buy one for the parking lot, a box for later.
6️⃣ 🍧 Shave Ice — Matsumoto, Haleiwa. Since 1951. Lilikoi + ice cream bottom + snow cap.
7️⃣ 🥞 Lilikoi Pancakes — Moke's, Kailua. Passion fruit in the batter, coconut syrup on top. Closes 1 PM!
8️⃣ 🥖 Pho French Dip — The Pig and the Lady, Chinatown. The cult sandwich with dipping broth.
9️⃣ 🍹 Monkeypod Mai Tai — Monkeypod Kitchen, Waikiki. Honey-lilikoi foam + sunset + live music.
🔟 🥥 Chocolate Haupia Cream Pie — Ted's Bakery, Sunset Beach. Eat a slice on the sand across the street.

💡 Pro tips: cluster by geography, go early (sellouts are real), and share the giants!
