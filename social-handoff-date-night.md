## Instagram Caption — "Your Oahu Guide"

🌅 DATE NIGHT ON OAHU? These 10 restaurants turn a nice evening into THE night you talk about for years 👇

From sunset hula to 10-seat omakase counters, here's where locals take the people they're trying to impress:

1️⃣ 🌺 House Without a Key, Waikiki — sunset hula under a century-old kiawe tree. The most romantic table in Waikiki, full stop.
2️⃣ 🍣 Sushi Sho, Waikiki — a 10-seat omakase inside the Ritz-Carlton Residences. Book months ahead; it's worth it.
3️⃣ ⚓ Merriman's Honolulu, Kaka'ako — harbor sunsets + mac nut-crusted mahi. 90% local ingredients.
4️⃣ 🏆 MW Restaurant, Kaka'ako — the James Beard date night. Save room for the Hawaiian chocolate dessert.
5️⃣ 🕯️ Fete, Chinatown — candlelit brick, open kitchen, and the best urban date night on the island.
6️⃣ 🍹 Mud Hen Water, Kaimuki — hyper-local menus + Hawaiian-ingredient cocktails. Low-key cool.
7️⃣ 🍷 Faria, Kailua — a tiny Portuguese wine bar that feels like a secret you discovered together.
8️⃣ 🌿 Haleiwa Joe's Haiku Gardens, Kaneohe — dinner inside an actual botanical garden. Book weeks ahead.
9️⃣ 🌊 Haleiwa Beach House, North Shore — sunset lanai, Kona lobster, and the Pacific as your wallpaper.
🔟 🎶 Monkeypod Kitchen, Waikiki — oceanfront mai tais with honey-lilikoi foam + live music nightly.

💡 Pro tips: book the sunset slot 30-60 min before golden hour, request the lanai/window table, and mention the occasion when you reserve.
