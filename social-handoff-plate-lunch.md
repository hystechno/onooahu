## Instagram Caption — "Your Oahu Guide"

🍚 TWO SCOOPS RICE. ONE SCOOP MAC SALAD. PURE ALOHA. The 10 best plate lunches on Oahu, vetted plate by plate 👇

The plate lunch is Hawaii's real everyday feast — born on the plantations, perfected at counters, trucks, and windows like these:

1️⃣ 🌈 Rainbow Drive-In, Kapahulu — the blueprint since 1961. Mix plate + loco moco under rich brown gravy.
2️⃣ 🏆 Helena's Hawaiian Food, Kalihi — James Beard-recognized Hawaiian food. The pipikaula short ribs are the legend.
3️⃣ 🌺 Highway Inn, Waipahu — the gold-standard Hawaiian plate (laulau, kalua pig, poi) since 1947.
4️⃣ 🐟 Yama's Fish Market, Moiliili — takeout-only lau lau plates + the famous haupia sweet potato pie.
5️⃣ ⛰️ Waiahole Poi Factory, Kaneohe — the Kanaka Nui plate with hand-pounded poi, made feet from your picnic table.
6️⃣ 🦐 Giovanni's Shrimp Truck, Haleiwa — the original garlic shrimp scampi plate. Messy, buttery, mandatory.
7️⃣ 🌊 Romy's Kahuku Prawns — prawns harvested from the ponds behind your table. Cash only, worth it.
8️⃣ ⚓ Nico's Pier 38, Kalihi — furikake pan-seared ahi, fresh off the morning fish auction next door.
9️⃣ 🍖 Side Street Inn, Kapahulu — where off-duty chefs eat. Pan-fried pork chops + a mountain of fried rice.
🔟 🕑 Zippy's, island-wide — the 24-hour Zip Pac. Hawaii's 2 AM safety net.

💡 Pro tips: bring cash, order the mix plate, and go early — the good stuff sells out.
