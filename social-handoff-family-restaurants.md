## Instagram Caption — "Your Oahu Guide"

🌺 OAHU WITH KIDS? These 10 restaurants make family meals actually easy 👇

Hawaii's food culture was built on casual counters and picnic tables — which means sandy feet and loud toddlers are welcome everywhere locals eat. Here are our 10 family favorites:

1️⃣ 🍧 Duke's Waikiki — toes-in-the-sand dinner + the legendary Hula Pie. Go at 5 PM for golden hour.
2️⃣ 🍜 Marukame Udon, Waikiki — kids watch noodles being made, then eat in under 5 minutes. Bowls from ~$7.
3️⃣ 🍱 Zippy's, island-wide — the every-generation answer. Zip Pac for kids, chili rice for parents, coco puffs for everyone.
4️⃣ 🌈 Rainbow Drive-In, Kapahulu — picnic tables + gravy-smothered mix plates since 1961. No shushing required.
5️⃣ 🦐 Giovanni's Shrimp Truck, North Shore — garlic shrimp with your hands, and kids can sign the truck!
6️⃣ 🌯 Kono's Northshore, Haleiwa — the breakfast bomber: one feeds a parent AND a kid.
7️⃣ 🛝 Farm to Barn, Haleiwa — lawn games + picnic tables + uala waffles. Kids play, parents finish their coffee.
8️⃣ 🍔 Seven Brothers, Haleiwa — oversized burgers and the famous coconut chocolate chip banana bread.
9️⃣ 🍧 Matsumoto Shave Ice, Haleiwa — the mandatory dessert stop since 1951. Rainbow + snow cap, trust us.
🔟 🌿 Waiahole Poi Factory, Kaneohe — real Hawaiian plate lunches at a 1905 poi factory. Let the kids try poi!

💡 Pro tips: eat early (5-6 PM), share the big plates, and save shave ice for last.
