## Instagram Caption — "Your Oahu Guide"

🍜 SKIP WAIKIKI. Eat where Honolulu actually eats — Kaimuki is Oahu's best food strip, and these 10 spots prove it 👇

Ten minutes from the beach, zero resort markup, all local flavor:

1️⃣ 🥟 Koko Head Cafe — the island's best brunch. Dumplings All Day Wong + cornflake French toast. Closed Tuesdays!
2️⃣ 🥂 Miro Kaimuki — French-Japanese prix fixe in the old Cafe Miro space. The ahi & uni brioche is the order.
3️⃣ 🌿 Mud Hen Water — Ed Kenney's hyper-local gem. Breadfruit gnocchi + Hawaiian-ingredient cocktails.
4️⃣ 🍲 Hale Vietnam — the pho that built a following. Order the large only if you're brave (and bring a layer — the AC is arctic).
5️⃣ 🌈 Rainbow Drive-In, Kapahulu — plate lunch blueprint since 1961. Mix plate + loco moco, ~$10-14.
6️⃣ 🐟 Ono Seafood — arguably the best poke on Oahu. Aku poke is the insider move. Sells out by mid-afternoon!
7️⃣ 🍱 Fresh Catch — 20+ poke varieties daily + the famous keawe-smoked meat with onions.
8️⃣ 🍩 Leonard's Bakery — hot malasadas since 1952. ~$2 each. Get the li hing.
9️⃣ 🍧 Waiola Shave Ice — the local answer to Matsumoto. Lilikoi + azuki bean bowl + snow cap.
🔟 🍖 Side Street Inn — where chefs eat after their shifts. Pan-fried pork chops until midnight.

💡 Pro tips: park once and walk, bring cash, and check closure days (Mondays & Tuesdays are tricky)!
